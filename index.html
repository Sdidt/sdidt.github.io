Siddharth Meenachi Sundaram

Hi everyone, I'm Siddharth, but you can call me Sid. I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, in the Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization (ACO) program. I am fortunate to be advised by Sahil Singla and Calum MacRury.

I am broadly interested in the design of online and approximation algorithms. More specifically, I have recently been thinking about problems of fair allocation of goods among buyers in a market.

Previously, I completed my Master's in Computer Science at Georgia Tech. Before that, I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, where I worked with Anwitaman Datta.
